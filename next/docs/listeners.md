description: "listen, queue, like and share on plyr.fm"
---

Anyone can listen without an account. Sign in with an atproto account to like
tracks, comment, build playlists and keep your queue across devices.

## listening

Play a track, an album or a playlist. The queue continues from what you
played.

## your library

Likes, playlists and albums you saved appear in your library.

## jams

A jam is a shared queue. Everyone in the jam hears the same track at the same
position.

## apps

plyr.fm runs in the browser and as an iOS app.
