---
title: "for artists"
description: "upload, publish and sell your music on plyr.fm"
---

Your account is the artist. What you publish is stored as records in your own
repository.

## uploading

Upload an audio file, add a title and artwork, and publish. Lossless files are
also served as MP3.

## albums

Group tracks into an album. Albums and playlists are the same record type.

## access

Each track or album is open or closed. Closed releases are playable by
supporters or buyers you choose.

## selling

Add support and purchase links in settings. They appear on your profile and
releases.
