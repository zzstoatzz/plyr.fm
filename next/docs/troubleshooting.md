---
title: "troubleshooting"
description: "common problems and what to check"
---

## sign-in

If sign-in fails, check that your PDS is reachable and try again.

## playback

If a track does not play, reload the page. A closed track plays only for
accounts with access.

## uploads

If an upload fails, check the file type and size. The upload page lists both.

## a change does not appear

Records reach plyr.fm through the network. A new record can take a few
seconds to appear.
